Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. Here's what you need to know.

⚠️
This action is permanent

Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered. Your account is shared across all One-Source apps (OneShake, OneSurp, One-Stay) — deleting it will remove access to all three.

What Will Be Deleted

When you delete your One-Stay account, the following data is permanently removed across all One-Source apps:

  • 👤 Your profile, username, display name, and avatar
  • 🗝️ All stay reviews, ratings, and notes
  • 📸 All uploaded photos (review images and profile picture)
  • 💬 All comments you've made on other reviews
  • 👥 Your friends list and all friend connections
  • 🔔 All notification history and preferences
  • 🏆 Your leaderboard rank and loves given/received

How to Delete Your Account

Send an email to our support team with your account details:

  1. Send an email to support@one-slurp.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete One-Stay Account"
  3. Include the email address associated with your account
  4. Optionally include your username so we can locate your account faster
✉️ Send Deletion Request
🕐

Account deletion requests are processed within 48 hours. You'll receive a confirmation email once your data has been removed.

Questions?

If you're having issues with the app, we'd love to help before you delete. Reach out to us at support@one-slurp.com and we'll do our best to resolve any concerns.
