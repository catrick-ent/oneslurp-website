Privacy Policy

How we collect and use your information

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Email address and password (for account creation)
  • Display name and username
  • Profile photo
  • Stay reviews, ratings, photos, and notes

Information collected automatically:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage data and interactions
  • Approximate location (for property search, with permission)
  • Push notification token (if notifications are enabled)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the OneStay service
  • To display your reviews and profile to other users
  • To send push notifications about activity on your reviews
  • To display relevant advertising via Google AdMob
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider
  • Google AdMob — for advertising (subject to Google's privacy policy)
  • Expo / EAS — for app delivery and push notifications
  • Law enforcement when required by law

4. Your Account is Shared Across OneSource Apps

OneStay uses the same account system as OneShake and OneSlurp. Your profile, friends, and account settings are shared across all three apps. Deleting your account removes access to all OneSource apps.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing support@one-slurp.com or visiting our Delete Account page.

6. Children's Privacy

OneStay is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at support@one-slurp.com.
